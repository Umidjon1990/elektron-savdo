Men POS tizimi qurayapman. Hozir “Mijozlar” sahifasi bor (UI: chapda sidebar, yuqorida “Mijozlar” sarlavhasi, qidiruv inputi, pastda jadval: Mijoz | Telefon | Jami Buyurtmalar | Jami Savdo | Oxirgi xarid). Shu sahifa juda sodda, uni professional “Customer/CRM” modulga kengaytirib ber.

TALAB: UI hozirgi dizaynga maksimal mos qolsin (sidebar, sarlavha, search, table). Lekin funksional quyidagi 4 modul bo‘lsin:

1. MIJOZLAR RO‘YXATI (search -> customer card)

* Qidiruv: telefon yoki ism bo‘yicha (instant search).
* Jadval ustunlari: Mijoz, Telefon, Jami Buyurtmalar, Jami Savdo, Oxirgi xarid, (qo‘shimcha) Qarzdorlik badge (agar qarzi bo‘lsa).
* Row bosilganda “Customer Card” (drawer yoki yangi page) ochilsin:

  * Asosiy info: Ism, telefon, manzillar (multiple), izoh.
  * Summary: total orders, total revenue, last purchase, outstanding debt.
  * Tabs: “Buyurtmalar”, “Yetkazib berish tarixi”, “Qarzlar/To‘lovlar”.
  * Actions: “Buyurtma yaratish” (ixtiyoriy), “Qarz to‘lovini qabul qilish”.

2. BUYURTMALAR (sayt orqali keladigan buyurtmalar ro‘yxati)

* Menu/route: /orders (yoki /buyurtmalar).
* Jadval ustunlari: OrderID, Mijoz, Telefon, Manzil, Summa, To‘lov turi (cash/card/online/debt), To‘lov holati (paid/partial/unpaid), Yetkazish turi (delivery/pickup), Status, Sana.
* Statuslar (minimal): NEW, CONFIRMED, PREPARING, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED, CANCELED.
* Filtrlar: sana oraliq (kun/hafta/oy/custom), status, to‘lov holati, yetkazish turi.
* Order detail page/modal:

  * Items list (name, qty, price)
  * Customer + address
  * Delivery assignment (courier)
  * Status update timeline (audit log)
  * “DELIVERED” bo‘lsa Delivery history va moliya (tushum/qarz)ga aks etsin.

3. YETKAZIB BERISH TARIXI (delivery history)

* Menu/route: /deliveries
* Jadval: Sana, OrderID, Mijoz, Manzil, Kuryer (kim), Qachon (scheduled time), Status (DELIVERED/FAILED/RETURNED/CANCELED), Izoh.
* Filtr: sana, kuryer, status.
* Har bir delivery order bilan bog‘langan bo‘lsin.
* Customer Card ichidagi tabda shu mijozning delivery tarixini ko‘rsat.

4. QARZDORLIK (debtors)

* Menu/route: /debtors
* Jadval: Mijoz, Telefon, Umumiy qarz, Oxirgi to‘lov sanasi, Keyingi to‘lov sanasi, Status (UPCOMING/OVERDUE), Action: “To‘lov qabul qilish”.
* Filtrlar: yaqin to‘lovchilar (3/7/14 kun), muddati o‘tganlar, qarz miqdori (min-max).
* Customer Card -> “Qarzlar/To‘lovlar” tab:

  * Qarzni keltirib chiqargan buyurtmalar ro‘yxati
  * To‘lovlar tarixi (qisman/to‘liq)
  * Qoldiq.

INTEGRATSIYA QOIDALARI (MUHIM):

* Qarzdorlik order bilan bog‘langan bo‘lsin (order.debt_amount yoki payment_status).
* Qisman to‘lov kiritilganda qoldiq avtomatik kamayishi kerak.
* Delivery status “DELIVERED” bo‘lganda order “completed” bo‘lib, moliyaga tushum/qarz yozilsin.
* Har bir update audit log (kim, qachon, nima o‘zgardi) bilan saqlansin.

TEXNIK TALABLAR:

* Menga to‘liq ishlaydigan kichik MVP tuzib ber:

  1. DB schema (SQLite/Postgres) + migration
  2. Backend API (CRUD): customers, orders, deliveries, debts, payments, audit_logs
  3. Frontend pages + components: CustomersList, CustomerCard, OrdersList, OrderDetail, DeliveriesList, DebtorsList
  4. Seed data (10-20 mijoz, 30 buyurtma, delivery + debt aralash)
  5. Qidiruv va filtrlar ishlasin (server-side yoki client-side, lekin tez va aniq)
  6. Pagination (kamida customers/orders)

UI/UX:

* Dizayn rasmga o‘xshasin: yuqorida search, pastda table, satrlar toza, badge rangli (qarzdorlik, status).
* Customer Card drawer bo‘lsa yaxshi (o‘ngdan ochiladigan), bo‘lmasa alohida /customers/:id page.
* Jadvalda telefon ikonka va kichik vizual elementlar bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin minimalistik.

YAKUNIY NATIJA:

* Loyihani ishga tushirish bo‘yicha qisqa instruktsiya yoz.
* Qaysi fayllarda nima borligini aniq ko‘rsat.
* Har bir modul uchun asosiy endpointlar ro‘yxatini ber.

Iltimos, avval struktura + schema + endpointlarni yoz, keyin kodni joylashtir va seed data bilan demo ishlashini ta’minla.
